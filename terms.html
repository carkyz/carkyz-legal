What Carkyz is

Not a substitute for your manufacturer's service schedule

Mileage estimates, interval calculations, and reminders are aids, not guarantees. They are based on data you enter and simple projections. Always follow your vehicle manufacturer's official service schedule and your own judgment. Carkyz is not responsible for missed maintenance, vehicle damage, or any loss arising from reliance on the app's estimates or reminders.

Free features

The core app — vehicle tracking, service reminders, mileage prediction, fuel journal, backup and restore — is free to use, with a limit of two vehicles.

Carkyz Pro (subscription)

Additional features (analytics, full expense tracking, unlimited vehicles, exported reports) are available through an auto-renewing subscription, offered as:

  • Monthly — billed every month.
  • Annual — billed every year, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

Prices are shown in the app and in the App Store / Google Play listing before you buy, and may vary by region. Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account at confirmation of purchase.

Auto-renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period, at the same price you agreed to (unless the price changed and you were notified in advance, as required by the App Store / Google Play). You can manage or cancel your subscription any time in your device's account settings (App Store: Settings → your name → Subscriptions; Google Play: Play Store app → Profile → Payments & subscriptions). Carkyz cannot cancel your subscription for you — that is handled entirely by Apple or Google.

Free trial

The 7-day free trial applies to the annual plan for new subscribers only. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the paid subscription begins automatically and payment is charged.

Refunds

All payments are processed by Apple or Google, and their refund policies apply. Carkyz does not process payments or issue refunds directly. Request a refund through reportaproblem.apple.com (Apple) or Google Play's own purchase history (Google).

What happens if you stop paying

If your subscription lapses, vehicles beyond the free limit are marked inactive rather than deleted — you keep read-only access to their service history at all times. Your data is never taken away for non-payment.

Restoring purchases

If you reinstall the app or switch devices, use the "Restore Purchases" button on the subscription screen to recover an active subscription tied to your Apple ID or Google account.
