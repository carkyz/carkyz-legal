What Carkyz is

Carkyz is an app for keeping track of your vehicles: mileage, service, fuel, expenses and documents. It estimates your mileage and reminds you when maintenance or a document is due. Your data is stored on your device; there is no Carkyz account and no Carkyz server holding your data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Estimates and reminders are aids, not guarantees

Mileage estimates, intervals and reminders are calculated from the data you enter. Always follow your vehicle manufacturer's official service schedule, the legal deadlines for your documents, and your own judgment. Carkyz is not responsible for missed maintenance or deadlines, vehicle damage, or any loss arising from reliance on the app's estimates or reminders.

Reading odometer and receipt photos

The app can read the mileage from a photo of the odometer and the amount and quantity from a photo of a fuel receipt. This happens on your phone and can be wrong or incomplete, for example with a blurry photo or an unusual receipt. Check the filled-in values before saving: what is saved is what you confirm.

Units and currency

Distance (km or miles), fuel volume (litres or gallons) and currency are labels you choose in the app. Changing them does not convert values you have already entered.

Your data and backups

Because your data stays on your device, we cannot recover it if the phone is lost or the app is deleted without a backup. You can save a backup file at any time, and restoring from a backup is always free. Automatic backup (Premium) saves copies to your own iCloud Drive (iPhone) or Google Drive (Android); the terms of Apple or Google apply to those services.

Free version

Carkyz is free to use for up to two vehicles, including service tracking and reminders, mileage estimates, reading the mileage from an odometer photo, the fuel log, the car passport, widgets, and backup to a file.

Reading fuel receipts is a Premium feature, but you can try it three times for free. A try is counted only when something was read from the photo. The counter is kept on your phone.

Carkyz Premium (one-time purchase)

Premium adds features on top of the free version. It currently includes: reading fuel receipts from a photo, more than two vehicles, documents and their deadlines (insurance, inspection, tax and others), all expenses with yearly totals, fuel charts, the vehicle report (PDF, Excel, CSV) and automatic backup. The Premium screen in the app shows the current list before you buy.

Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription: you pay once, nothing renews, and there is nothing to cancel. The price is shown in the app and in the App Store / Google Play before you buy and may vary by region. Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account when you confirm the purchase.

Premium does not expire. It stays with your Apple ID or Google account for as long as Carkyz is available for your device. New features may be added to Premium over time.

  • Automatic backup pauses. Copies already saved stay in your iCloud Drive or Google Drive, and you can still save and restore backup files.

If you reinstall the app or switch devices, use Restore purchases on the Premium screen to recover Premium bought with your Apple ID or Google account. Restoring is free.
